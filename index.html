MERI CHHOTI BEHEN KE LIYE

Sun meri chhoti behen…

Main tumse dil se maafi maangta hoon. Maine jo bhi galat kaha, jis tarah daanta, ya jis wajah se tumhe laga ki main tumhe samajhta nahi — uske liye sorry.

Us waqt main khud bahut kuch jhel raha tha. Jis insaan ko apni niyat saaf lagti ho, uske liye aise allegations sunna bahut takleefdeh ho sakta hai. Main bhi usi takleef, gusse aur confusion mein uljha hua tha. Lekin mujhe apne dard ko tum par nahi nikalna chahiye tha.

Main tumhe apna maanta hoon, isliye tumhari fikr karta hoon. Par fikr ka matlab tumhe daantna, tumhari baat ko nazarandaaz karna, ya tumhare decisions control karna nahi hona chahiye. Main yeh samajhne ki koshish kar raha hoon.

Rudra aur baaki logon ko lekar meri jo bhi soch hai, main chahta hoon tum use ek baar suno, lekin aakhri faisla tum apni samajh se karna. Main tumhe kisi se rishta todne ke liye majboor nahi karna chahta. Bas itna chahta hoon ki tum apni izzat, safety aur khushi ka khayal rakho.

Agar tum mujhse naraz ho, toh tumhara naraz hona valid hai. Agar tumhe waqt chahiye, main uska bhi respect karunga. Tumhe abhi jawab dene ya mujhe maaf karne ki koi majboori nahi.

Main nahi chahta ki meri galtiyon ki wajah se tum socho ki mere dil mein tumhare liye nafrat hai. Aisa bilkul nahi hai. Kabhi-kabhi insaan apne dard mein itna ulajh jaata hai ki apno ko dard dene lagta hai — aur phir samajh aata hai ki apne dard ko samjhana zaroori tha, apno par utaarna nahi. Tum mere liye sirf ek chhoti behen nahi, meri zindagi ka ek apna hissa ho. Main chahta hoon ki waqt ke saath meri baatein nahi, mera badla hua bartav tumhe yeh mehsoos karaye.
